Sound Advice — music credits
============================

Everything except the two files below is generated by code inside the
app (filtered noise), so there is nothing to credit and nothing to
license.

To add the two music conditions, put files here named exactly:

    classical.mp3
    lyrical.mp3

and fill in the details below. Both MUST be either public domain or
Creative Commons — a commercial track cannot be used, even for a
school project, because the app is published on the open web.

Where to find them
------------------
Classical : musopen.org — filter for "Public Domain" recordings.
            The composition being old is not enough; the RECORDING
            must be public domain too.
Lyrical   : freemusicarchive.org — filter licence to CC BY or CC BY-SA.
            Pick something with clear English vocals, since the point
            of the condition is that the words compete for attention.

Fill this in before you launch
------------------------------

classical.mp3
  Title      :
  Performer  :
  Composer   :
  Source URL :
  Licence    :

lyrical.mp3
  Title      :
  Artist     :
  Source URL :
  Licence    :

CC BY legally requires you to credit the artist. The app shows this
information on its Credits screen, so filling it in is not optional.
