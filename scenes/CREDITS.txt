Sound Advice — concentration test image credits
===============================================

The 20 photographs in this folder are the scenes used by the go/no-go
concentration test: 10 cities (go trials) and 10 mountains (no-go).

They are not shipped as downloaded. tools/prepare-scenes.py square-crops
each one, converts it to grayscale, and rescales it so every image has
the same mean brightness and the same contrast as every other. See
SOURCES.txt for which original became which scene file.

Licence check before adding any photo
-------------------------------------
Only free-licence photos. Unsplash, Pexels and Wikimedia Commons are all
fine. Watch out for "Unsplash+" — that is Unsplash's PAID tier, and those
images carry faint tiled watermark text that is nearly invisible on
screen but shows up clearly once contrast is matched. On Unsplash, a
small "+" badge on the thumbnail means skip it.

Neither Unsplash nor Pexels legally requires attribution, but crediting
the photographers is the right thing to do and it is one more thing the
judges can see you thought about.

The paradigm itself
-------------------
The scene-categorisation idea comes from the GradCPT, developed by the
Boston Attention Lab and distributed by The Many Brains Project. These
photographs are our own — the original GradCPT image set is theirs and
is not used here. Cite Fortenbaugh et al. (2015), Psychological Science
26(9), 1497–1510.
